# Colorado admissions re-research: differences from the live records (2026-11-24)

The re-research (tools/state-build/ADMISSIONS_BRIEF.md) found these differences between what each school publishes now and the
program records the site shows today. The owner approved correcting the live records (2026-11-24): program cards now show the re-researched details from
`CO.admissions`, and 20 outdated or missing deadlines were updated in the program records.

## Trinidad State College: Associate Degree Nursing (Nursing AAS) (`trinidad-state-adn`)

The existing record states a 3.0 prerequisite GPA and a 2.5 cumulative GPA as minimums. The page lists these GPAs only among the point-system criteria, not clearly as cutoffs, so both GPA fields are set to see_text. The page doesn't state minimum prerequisite grades, the HESI cutoff, the point maximum or the tie rule.

## Pikes Peak State College: Associate Degree in Nursing (AAS) (`pikes-peak-adn`)

The existing record is correct but incomplete. The scoring sheet also requires a CCCS CNA course or a Colorado CNA license, and an Information Night Attestation Form. Applicants must be 18 at the time of application. TEAS is limited to 2 attempts in 12 months, and scores must be within 365 days. Non-BIO general education prerequisites must be within 15 years. The first two BIO attempts are averaged, with no more than one D/F in 7 years.

## Pueblo Community College: Associate Degree Nursing (AAS) (`pueblo-cc-adn`)

Matches the existing record. Details to add: the Fall 2027 application opens April 1, 2027 and closes May 26, 2027. TEAS is limited to twice in 18 months, and results are viable for 18 months. Retakes use the better of the first two attempts. Points are also awarded for courses taken at PCC (2 points each), health-related work hours and a prior degree.

## Front Range Community College: Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Nursing (`front-range-adn`)

The existing record calls selection 'competitive ranking'. FRCC publishes a 100-point ranking table, so selection is now 'points'. HESI A-2 now includes a new Critical Thinking subject. Applicants who tested before August 2026 must take Critical Thinking separately; the 2025-2026 ranking table (updated 8/3/26) still lists only four subjects. HESI may be taken twice in 365 days and is valid for 15 months. Ties go by order received. Two D/F grades in the same course make an applicant ineligible. The 10-year limit applies to science prerequisites specifically.
