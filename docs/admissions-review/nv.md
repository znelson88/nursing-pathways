# NV admissions re-research: differences from the earlier records

What each school publishes now, compared with the program records the site showed before. Cards now show the
re-researched details (NV.admissions in data/schools/nv.js). Closures or paused admissions are flagged here,
never removed automatically.

## Research of 2026-11-26

### College of Southern Nevada: Nursing (AAS) - Associate Degree Nursing (`csn-adn`)

No substantive conflict with the existing record (GPA 2.50, TEAS 75/75/70/75 with 75/60/60/60 exception, 5-year TEAS validity, March 1, 2027 deadline, up to 112 Regular / 24 Extended seats all confirmed). New detail: Spring 2028 Regular advising sheet (dated 7/2026, linked on the program page under the sp27 filename) lists a September 1, 2027 deadline and up to 112 seats. Retake rule (3 attempts max; highest grade used for GPA; points only for B or better in first attempt) not in the existing record.

### College of Southern Nevada: LPN to RN Bridge (AAS) (`csn-adn-lpn-bridge`)

No change from the existing record. Advising sheet still dated 5/2025.

### College of Southern Nevada: Bachelor of Science in Nursing - RN to BSN (`csn-rnbsn`)

Existing deadline omits the Spring Accelerated entry: the Spring advising sheet (dated 9/30/2026) lists Spring 2027 deadline Dec. 9, 2026 and Spring 2028 deadline Dec. 8, 2027; Fall 2028 deadline July 12, 2028 also posted. The RN to BSN page now links only Accelerated Track sheets (Fall and Spring); the Extended Track sheet (1/2025, Jan 10 deadline) is not linked there, so the existing 'Extended (Spring entry): January 10' may be outdated; confirm with the school.

### Truckee Meadows Community College: Nursing, AS (Associate Degree in Nursing) (`tmcc-adn`)

Nothing could be verified on the school's site, so no changes can be confirmed. Unverified search excerpts (not usable as values) of tmcc.edu pages broadly match the existing record (MATH 124+, BIOL 223/224/251 done before applying; 2.75 lower-division GPA, 3.00 science prerequisite GPA; science courses within 5 years, max two attempts; HESI administered by TMCC, 75% each section, scores valid 1 year, two attempts in 12 months, only sections below 75% may be retaken; ranked by HESI and GPA, each weighted 50%, HESI average / 25 = points). The steps-to-apply page is headed 'Nursing Program Application Scoring Rubric Changes Effective January 2027'; the new rubric could not be read, so the ranking method may change for upcoming cycles. BLOCKED: every tmcc.edu page tried returned HTTP 403 to automated reading (nursing/adn/steps-to-apply, nursing/adn/application, nursing/faq, nursing/steps-to-apply/pre-admission-testing), and catalog.tmcc.edu refused connections (nursing-as page and nursing-as.pdf). All fields are therefore not_published; the existing record's facts should stay flagged low-confidence until checked by hand. The catalog PDF (Last Generated 09/09/2026) and steps-to-apply page are the pages to verify manually.

### Truckee Meadows Community College: RN to BSN, Bachelor of Science in Nursing (`tmcc-rnbsn`)

Nothing could be verified on the school's site; no changes confirmed. BLOCKED: https://www.tmcc.edu/nursing/rn-bsn/steps-to-apply returned HTTP 403 and catalog.tmcc.edu refused connections. Existing record (RN license, ADN from accredited program, Summer 2026 cycle Dec 1, 2025 - June 30, 2026) remains unverified; check by hand.

### Western Nevada College: Nursing - Associate of Applied Science (Associate Degree Nursing Program) (`wnc-adn`)

No substantive changes vs existing record. Refinements: catalog says STAT 152 is acceptable for math (modeled as an alternative); prerequisites may be in progress at application if completed by end of spring semester. The Nursing Application 2026 PDF linked from the nursing page now returns 404, so the TEAS score-to-points bands could not be verified on a live page.

### Great Basin College: Associate of Applied Science in Nursing (AAS Nursing) (`gbc-adn`)

Mostly confirmed. NURS 130 is not in the catalog's prerequisite list; the requirement is a current Nevada CNA license (moved to notes). STAT 152 split out as an alternative to MATH. Retakes: highest grade counts. The program page still links the Fall 2025 criteria PDF; the Fall 2026 PDF is the newest found. The catalog also mentions extra points for CTE pathway students, which the Fall 2026 points sheet does not list.

### Great Basin College: Paramedic/LPN Bridge Pathway to the AAS in Nursing (`gbc-adn-lpn-bridge`)

Confirmed: prerequisites, 16-point maximum and residency/veteran points match. Added: highest grade counts for repeated courses. Tie-break rule is not published. The Summer 2027 PDF text refers to Spring 2026 grades, likely a carry-over typo.

### Great Basin College: RN to BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing) (`gbc-rnbsn`)

Confirmed: 3.0 GPA, provisional 2.5-3.0 with 2.7 first semester, points system, fall only, and the conflicting Aug 1 vs July 1 deadlines. Added the 2020 points form (max 16); it does not list the essay that the catalog mentions.

### Nevada State University: Nursing, B.S. (Pre-Licensure) (`nevada-state-bsn`)

Existing record matches the 2026-2027 catalog on GPA (2.5 cumulative / 3.25 program-specific), TEAS 60% overall and per section within 2 years, BIOL 223 before applying, 14-credit in-progress cap, 7-year biology limit, two attempts in 5 years, and the three priority windows. New details: TEAS has no attempt limit but needs 30 days between attempts; program-specific GPA is computed from prerequisite courses only; the school's 2024-2025 Pre-Nursing Review (updated 7/18/24, still hosted) publishes a 1,000-point ranking rubric, whereas the existing record says the site doesn't describe ranking. Only the most recent prerequisite grade counts.

### Nevada State University: Nursing, B.S. (RN-BSN Online) (`nevada-state-rnbsn`)

Matches the existing record. Refinement: the 2026-2027 handbook says the license must be active and unencumbered 'in the state in which they reside and/or in the state where the student's practice experiences will occur', and that state must be on the program's approved-states list (existing record says only state of residence).

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN), Traditional 16-month track (`unlv-bsn`)

Mostly matches the existing record (3.0 GPA, B in MATH 124/KIN 223/KIN 224/BIOL 251/NURS 299, C in other prerequisites, up to 104 per term, Feb/Jun/Oct application months). Added from the calculation worksheet still hosted on UNLV's site (dated 8.20.19): points structure, HESI A2 75% in all areas with two attempts, two-attempt rule for B courses, four-application limit, 88-point minimum; these are older and may not be current. Retake rule found: UNLV uses the most recent grade. No 2026-27 BSN worksheet is posted yet.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Master of Science in Nursing, Direct Entry Generalist (DEMSN) Track (`unlv-demsn`)

No change found. Prerequisite list and GPA still could not be confirmed: the catalog (catalog.unlv.edu) returns 403, the program page and MSN handbook (2026-08) defer to the catalog, and the 2026-27 plan of study lists only program courses. For applicants with a bachelor's degree in a field other than nursing. 48 credits, 12-month full-time program with a 16-month, four-semester option; over 500 clinical hours in Las Vegas. International applications "currently not accepted"; out-of-state applicants eligible in select states only. Search-engine excerpts of an older catalog mention a 2.5 bachelor's GPA, a 3.0 prerequisite GPA, and courses such as microbiology, A&P I and II, nutrition, lifespan development, statistics, psychology, sociology and college algebra, but these could not be read on the school's site; confirm with nursing.demsn@unlv.edu or 702-895-3360.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Master of Science in Nursing, Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) Track (`unlv-fnp`)

No change found; GPA 3.0, no GRE, Feb. 1 fall deadline confirmed. Requires a BSN from an accredited program and "an active, unencumbered and unrestricted nursing license in the state where you will be doing your clinical hours/practicums." Guaranteed clinical placements in the Las Vegas metro area for students with a valid Nevada RN license. 41 credits minimum (2026-27 plan of study). Course prerequisites, references and experience rules are in the graduate catalog, which returned 403.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Master of Science in Nursing, Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) Track (`unlv-pmhnp`)

The note "MSN-PMHNP start is contingent upon class size" no longer appears on the Graduate Studies page (checked 2026-11-26); the existing record still cites it. Otherwise no change. GPA and GRE come from UNLV's general MSN FAQ (which applies to the MSN program), not a PMHNP-specific page. BSN and an active, unencumbered, unrestricted RN license in the state of clinical practicums required. The MSN degree page lists the PMHNP track as "Available Online"; the separate BSN-to-DNP PMHNP track is "On Hold". The track page defers admission details to the graduate catalog, which returned 403.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Master of Science in Nursing, Certified Nurse-Midwifery (CNM) Track (`unlv-cnm`)

No change found. GPA and GRE come from UNLV's general MSN FAQ, not a midwifery-specific page. BSN and an active, unencumbered, unrestricted RN license in the state of clinical practicums required; the track page adds that a Nevada RN license may be necessary for clinical training. 53 credits minimum (2026-27 plan of study). Admission details are deferred to the graduate catalog, which returned 403.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Doctor of Nursing Practice, Post-Bachelor's Family Nurse Practitioner (BSN-DNP FNP) (`unlv-fnp-dnp`)

No change found. DNP degree page lists the Post Bachelor's Family Nurse Practitioner Track as "Available Online". For bachelor's-prepared RNs (BSN and RN license). Admission requirements (GPA, license wording, experience) are only in the graduate catalog, which returned 403; the DNP handbook (Fall 2026 - Summer 2027) defers admission to the catalog. 68 credits minimum (2026-27 plan of study). Contact gradnursingadmissions@unlv.edu or 702-895-3360.

### University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), School of Nursing: Doctor of Nursing Practice, Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist (CRNA) Track (`unlv-crna`)

No change found. The track page still has Apply links, the Fall 2026 - Summer 2027 DNP handbook includes CRNA program sections, and the DNP degree page lists the CRNA track with no status label (the BSN-to-DNP PMHNP track is "On Hold"). For baccalaureate-prepared RNs; 36-month hybrid curriculum with the first semester fully online; 91 credits minimum (2026-27 plan of study). GPA, critical-care experience, CCRN and references are not stated on readable school pages (the catalog returned 403). Search-engine excerpts of an older catalog mention a 3.2 BSN GPA and one year (2,000 hours) of RN practice; these could not be confirmed. Contact CRNA Program Director James Stimpson via gradnursingadmissions@unlv.edu.

### Roseman University of Health Sciences (College of Nursing): Accelerated Bachelor of Science in Nursing (ABSN) (`roseman-bsn`)

No substantive change from the existing record (prerequisites, 2.75 GPA, TEAS 58.7% within 12 months, max 3 attempts per calendar year with 4 weeks between, competitive with interview and writing sample, same deadlines). Added detail: applications accepted with coursework in progress; credit-by-exam science courses not accepted. The on-campus cohort term conflict (Fall 2027 on program page vs Summer 2027 on apply page) persists.

### Roseman University of Health Sciences (College of Nursing): Master of Science in Nursing - Family Nurse Practitioner / Doctor of Nursing Practice - Family Nurse Practitioner (MSNFNP/DNPFNP) (`roseman-fnp`)
