# AZ admissions re-research: differences from the earlier records

What each school publishes now, compared with the program records the site showed before. Cards now show the
re-researched details (AZ.admissions in data/schools/az.js). Closures or paused admissions are flagged here,
never removed automatically.

## Research of 2026-11-24

### Arizona State University (Edson College): Traditional Prelicensure BSN (`asu-bsn`)

Existing record says a "3.50 minimum critical-course GPA" and that ASU does not publish a course list. The Nursing Information Packet (updated Jan 2026) lists the 12 prerequisites with codes; competitive advancement needs a minimum 3.00 Prerequisite GPA (below 3.00 may get individual review) and TEAS of at least 58.7%. The 3.50 figure is the continuing-eligibility rule for first-year Direct Admission students only. Selection is a published 50% GPA / 50% TEAS advancement score with random tie-break, not "Select GPA."

### Arizona State University (Edson College): Accelerated BSN (post-baccalaureate) (`asu-absn`)

Existing record has no GPA or exam. The packet requires a minimum 3.00 Prerequisite GPA (individual review possible below), TEAS at least 58.7%, 10 listed prerequisites, and ranks by a 50% GPA / 50% TEAS advancement score. The ~70 credit hours is a minimum before start, not an alternative to the degree; first-degree students may also be considered.

### Arizona State University (Edson College): MS in Nursing — Entry to Nursing Practice (MEPN direct-entry) (`asu-demsn`)

Existing record lists only "a bachelor's degree in another field" with no GPA, exam or deadlines. The MS Application Guide (updated August 2026) requires 3.00 GPA in last 60 hours, 3.0 prerequisite GPA, 8 prerequisites, TEAS 58.7% minimum, and gives Summer 2027 deadlines. Applicants who already hold a BSN are not eligible. Existing source_url (MEPN-ABSN-campaign) is a marketing page; the program page now redirects to degrees.asu.edu.

### Arizona State University (Edson College): Online RN-to-BSN (`asu-rnbsn`)

Adds the C-or-higher requirement in ENG 101, ENG 102 and an approved college math course. The specific "not available to residents of TN or WA" wording was not seen; the page now says "Reside in the U.S. (including some state restrictions)" and points to the state authorization page.

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Family Nurse Practitioner) (`asu-fnp`)

Existing record calls selection "competitive"; the page says applications are reviewed on a space-available basis with priority for those complete by the priority deadline, and that an admission exam is not required. Otherwise consistent (3.00 GPA last 60 hours; nursing degree; unencumbered RN license).

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Adult-Gerontology Acute Care NP) (`asu-agacnp`)

Existing source_url is the general DNP index; the program page is on degrees.asu.edu. Selection is space-available after a priority deadline rather than "competitive"; no admission exam. The page's degree sentence reads "a bachelor's or master's degree in from a regionally accredited institution" (the word "nursing" appears to be missing, a typo on the page).

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Adult-Gerontology Nurse Practitioner) (`asu-agpcnp`)

Existing source_url is the general DNP index; program page is degrees.asu.edu (NUANPAGDNP). Selection is space-available after the Dec 15 priority deadline rather than "competitive"; no admission exam.

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Pediatric Nurse Practitioner) (`asu-pnp`)

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Psychiatric Mental Health NP) (`asu-pmhnp`)

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Women's Health Nurse Practitioner) (`asu-whnp`)

### Arizona State University (Edson College): DNP — Advanced Nursing Practice (Nurse Anesthesia) (`asu-crna`)

Existing source_url is the general DNP index. New details: 3.20 science GPA minimum; A&P (two semesters), general chemistry and statistics prerequisites within 7 years; two years full-time RN experience before matriculation and one year full-time critical care (at least 1,800 hours, generally within 3 years) before application. The page says the program is pending COA accreditation and first-cohort offers are conditional on that outcome. Priority deadline Jan 15.

### University of Arizona College of Nursing: Bachelor of Science in Nursing — Conventional Pathway (`u-arizona-bsn`)

Existing record gives GPA only as transfer/change-of-major 3.6; the Advanced Standing (Continuing Eligibility) rule is a minimum cumulative 3.00 in all college-level coursework, each prerequisite B or better, no more than three prerequisites in progress at the conference, eligibility within five semesters. Prerequisite list itself unchanged (composition, chemistry, A&P I/II, intro biology, microbiology, statistics, lifespan, nutrition). Placement runs through the September/February Advanced Standing Process, which the existing deadline omits.

### University of Arizona College of Nursing: BSN — Integrative Health Accelerated Pathway (`u-arizona-absn`)

Existing deadline omits the September/February Advanced Standing Process and the December/May change-of-major windows.

### University of Arizona College of Nursing: Master of Science — Entry to the Profession of Nursing (MEPN) (`u-arizona-demsn`)

Deadlines are now published (existing record null). Direct admit requires 3.00 cumulative and 3.50 prerequisite GPA, bachelor's within 10 years from ASU/NAU/U of A/GCU.

### Northern Arizona University (Sanghi College of Nursing): Bachelor of Science in Nursing (Traditional / Compressed) (`nau-bsn`)

### Northern Arizona University (Sanghi College of Nursing): BSN — Accelerated Option (second-degree) (`nau-absn`)

### Northern Arizona University (Sanghi College of Nursing): MS in Nursing — Advanced Practice (Psychiatric Mental Health NP) (`nau-pmhnp`)

### Grand Canyon University: Bachelor of Science in Nursing (Pre-Licensure) (`gcu-bsn`)

Existing record is outdated: it says no separate prerequisite list, entrance exam 'None stated' and only the general university GPA (2.8 / 2.5 + ACT/SAT). GCU's Fast-Track (main campus) BSN requires a secondary nursing application with 6 prerequisites + 9 GE courses, prerequisite GPA 3.25, programmatic GPA 3.0, HESI A2 (80/80/75), weighted stack-ranking, and max two secondary attempts. Also new: the PL-BSN admits only students whose external college coursework started before their high-school graduation (others are directed to the ABSN).

### Grand Canyon University: Accelerated BSN (ABSN) — Phoenix (`gcu-absn`)

Existing record lists entrance exam 'None stated' and no prerequisite courses; GCU requires HESI A2 (80/80/75), a 3.25 prerequisite GPA, and six prerequisite courses (C or higher within 7 years) plus GE courses. Cumulative 3.0 GPA and 60-credit/bachelor's requirement match.

### Grand Canyon University: MSN — Family Nurse Practitioner (`gcu-fnp`)

Existing GPA text 'Minimum GPA, or GRE/GMAT scores' is too loose: the handbook requires a cumulative 3.0 for MSN NP candidates and says APRN applicants cannot be accepted with specifications. New experience rule: one year full-time RN work within the last 12 months related to the specialty, and current full-time employment in direct patient care. The existing source_url (online-degrees/fnp) is a marketing page; the program page is msn-family-nurse-practitioner.

### Grand Canyon University: MSN — Acute Care NP (Adult-Gerontology emphasis) (`gcu-agacnp`)

Existing GPA text 'Minimum GPA, or GRE/GMAT scores' conflicts with the handbook (cumulative 3.0; APRN applicants cannot be accepted with specifications). Experience rule is more specific than the existing record: current full-time hospital employment with at least one year of ICU, Adult ER, Progressive Care/Post-Op, Med-Surg, Interventional Radiology, LTAC, hospital dialysis or trauma experience.

### Grand Canyon University: MSN — Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (`gcu-pmhnp`)

Existing GPA text 'Minimum GPA, or GRE/GMAT scores' conflicts with the handbook (cumulative 3.0; APRN cannot be accepted with specifications). ### MaricopaNursing (Maricopa Community Colleges): Associate in Applied Science (AAS) in Nursing (`maricopa-adn`)

Math prerequisite: Scottsdale CC's March 2026 guides say MAT141 or higher (existing record says MAT 140/141/142; not re-verified on the blocked district page). Biology and chemistry prerequisites may be met with 1 year of high school biology/chemistry (chemistry code is CHM130AA). Corequisites also include CRE101 (or exemption) and a Humanities course. HESI scores valid 2 years (new). EMCC's FAQ quotes a wait of about 7-8 semesters (existing record says about 2-3 semesters). Nurse Assistant pause (July 1, 2025 - June 30, 2027) confirmed.

### Pima Community College: Nursing (ADN), AAS (`pima-adn`)

Existing record lists WRT 101 as a prerequisite row; Pima says WRT101 is no longer required to apply (taken in the first block) - remove it from prerequisites. Points rubric now published (max 100, revised 10/01/2026), including Accuplacer Reading, Accuplacer Advanced Algebra & Functions and CLEP College Composition points. NLN attempt limit (two in 12 months) and one-year validity are new details. Grades and deadlines match.

### Central Arizona College: Nursing, Associate of Applied Science (AAS) — RN Traditional (`central-arizona-adn`)

HESI minimums now per section (Reading Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Math 80%; A&P 70%) with a 5-attempt limit and 30-day wait; consistent with existing note. ENG 101/MAT 141 are registration eligibility only (not completed courses), so they are left out of prerequisites. BIO 205 is a co-requisite (Block 3), not an admission prerequisite. The application packet includes a points form, but its scoring is not published online, so selection is recorded as competitive rather than points.

### Eastern Arizona College: Nursing, Associate of Applied Science (AAS) (`eastern-arizona-adn`)

Existing record says TEAS cost $87 and Proficient; still true. New 2026-27 guide adds: TEAS within two years of application, limit of two attempts per semester and four per year; an EAC reading competency test (Accuplacer Next Gen 263 / TEAS Reading 72.4% / associate degree) is required to apply; credential may be an LNA, a Certified/Registered Medical Assistant, or NREMT-Paramedic. Points scale and seat count come from the 2025-26 FAQ (the 2026-27 guide does not repeat them).

### Mohave Community College: Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Nursing (`mohave-adn`)

Prerequisite list now adds MAT 101 (or higher) and PSY 101 or PSY 245; BIO 205 is listed as a required prerequisite. TEAS Proficient-or-higher applies beginning Fall 2027 (matches existing). Existing record's GPA and deadlines confirmed.

### Yavapai College: Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Nursing (`yavapai-adn`)

Statistics (MAT 167) is NOT an AAS prerequisite; the guide says it is required only for students pursuing the BSN. Existing list was missing ENG 102, MAT 141/142, BIO 156 or BIO 181, and PSY 101 or PHI 204. New detail: math/science within 10 years; TEAS valid 6 months, once per cycle, must be taken at YC.

### Yavapai College: RN-to-BSN (bachelor's completion) (`yavapai-rnbsn`)

None of substance; confirmed automatic acceptance of YC AAS graduates pending NCLEX-RN and licensure.

### Arizona Western College: Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Nursing (`arizona-western-adn`)

Confirmed existing record (2.5 GPA, HESI 75%, 445 points, top 50, ties by date then lottery). New detail: HESI within 24 months, retest after 60 days up to 3 times per 12 months; information session within 24 months; 10 extra readmission seats; 20 alternates. Prerequisites may be in progress at application.

### Coconino Community College: Nursing (AAS Degree) (`coconino-adn`)

Major differences from existing record: the current page names the ATI TEAS (minimum composite 66.1%), not the NLN NEX 50th percentile; admission is once per year for fall (applications due in May), not Fall and Spring; about 30 students per year, not up to 20 each Fall and Spring; the 3.0 GPA covers prerequisite and co-requisite courses. The old deadline (Spring 2027: September 4, 2026) is no longer shown. Older PDFs (tip sheets, application instructions) now return 404. Search results still show older NEX/TEAS figures that conflict; I used only the live page.

### Cochise College: Nursing, Associate of Applied Science (AAS) (`cochise-adn`)

2026-27 Form D adds PSY 101 (Introduction to Psychology) and PSY 240 (Developmental Psychology) to the prerequisite list (27 credits), which the existing record lacks. Form E says 100 openings. TEAS score valid two years; 2 attempts per application period (May 1-April 30).

### Northland Pioneer College: Registered Nursing (RN), AAS (`northland-pioneer-adn`)

ENL 102 and PSY 240 are now only strongly recommended, not required prerequisites (existing record lists them). New: math competency (QAS 250, ACT 21, or MAT 142+ with C within one year); science courses within 8 years; HESI no older than 18 months; application deadline February 6, 2027. 3.0 GPA remains preferred, not required.

### Creighton University (Phoenix): Accelerated BSN (ABSN) — Phoenix (`creighton-absn`)

Existing record lists 'Statistics, nutrition, ethics, psychology (and others)' as one row; the full list adds sociology/anthropology and developmental psychology, with credit-hour minimums. New: anatomy, physiology, chemistry and microbiology must be within 10 years; two of chemistry/anatomy/physiology must be done by the application deadline; all prerequisites done 45 days before start. Intro/survey chemistry and combined GOB chemistry do not count.
