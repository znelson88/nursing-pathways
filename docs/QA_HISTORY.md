# User testing history (career map)

Three rounds of simulated user studies, 20 people each, with the numbers measured on the real page
(Playwright on phone, laptop, 200% zoom and dark mode). Round 2 and 3 used the same mix of people:
3 high school seniors, 3 parents of college students, 4 career changers, 3 CNAs/LPNs, 2 people over 55,
1 nursing student, 1 non-native English speaker, 1 low-vision user, 1 color-blind user, 1 skeptic;
13 on phones, 7 on laptops.

## Round 1 → changes
Question-first welcome, plain-language labels, list view, and the move from a radial web of lines to the
six-step tiered map.

## Round 2 → changes (all but "Compare")
Forgiving search (typos, Spanish, everyday words), "Fits my schedule" asks before filtering, each job
shows one common route, RN no longer draws 31 lines, 12px minimum text, popups stay on screen,
"No nights" instead of strikethrough, the 31 "paid like RNs" jobs grouped in the pay ranking.
A phone bottom sheet was tried and then removed at the owner's request; replaced by a "↓ details below"
pill plus Reset map buttons.

## Round 3 → changes (all but the phone inline summary card)
- Search answers topics: loans/scholarships → aid section; NCLEX → license info; compact license →
  practice tab; state names set the state; abbreviations (ABSN, L&D, OR, WOC, corpsman, medic).
  Before: 13 of 60 test searches found nothing and 8 put the wrong answer first.
- No sideways scrolling at 200% zoom.
- Money tab starts with an "In short" box; details folded into sections (about 3,400 → 500 visible characters).
- Phone: filters moved into My situation; the map starts on the first screen.
